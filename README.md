> **TL;DR** — On a frozen panel of 52 open-weight checkpoints spanning 28 weight lineages and 11 architecture families, the first-decoding-step logit-gap margin -- 80 forward passes, no generation, no judge, no benchmark and no reference model -- predicts judged plain-harmful refusal rate at Spearman rho = 0.694 [0.495, 0.822] (member unit) and 0.564 [0.140, 0.826] (lineage unit), transfers across provenance blocks (0.667 vs 0.668), is not a parameter-count proxy (partial 0.676), and beats a reimplemented activation scanner by +0.421 [0.169, 0.684]. The same panel retracts two of the authors' earlier positives: an AMS paraphrase refit collapses from +0.296 at 7 lineages to +0.099 [-0.027, 0.244] at 28 with the effect localised to the original block, and a claimed read-versus-act coupling of 0.629 is shown by an exact variance decomposition to be 89.6% a between-axis-type contrast (within-axis 0.547, CI covering zero). The paper additionally quantifies an n-asymmetric verdict rule, breaks a label-axis lexical circularity (READS_CANONICAL_WORDING_ONLY), runs the one published leakage control on its own headline (-0.0205), and names the three measurement pathologies involved as instances rather than discoveries.

<summary>Full hypothesis</summary>

The project's object of study is unchanged (autoregressive generation and its activation geometry; goal = a cheap, benchmark-free safety score for arbitrary open-weight checkpoints). Iterations 1-4 retired the bistable/EWS mechanism, alpha_50 as a safety score, the five-check falsification protocol as a contribution, and BOTH iteration-3 positives (the AMS paraphrase refit and the induce-without-detect dissociation). Iteration 5 delivered the first positive: the first-decoding-step logit-gap harmful margin HOLDS at panel scale. What iteration 6 must deliver is (i) the correction of the panel figures attached to that score, which the artifact reports as 47 members / 24 lineages / 9 families and the draft over-states as 52/28/11; (ii) the two free, decisive tests that decide whether the score is a real predictor or a near-tautology - a prompt-disjoint criterion and the greedy-argmax baseline from the same 80 forward passes; (iii) an estimate of the outcome variable's own reliability ceiling, without which rho = 0.694 cannot be read; and (iv) a deployment-grade statement (decision threshold with precision/recall, a lexicon-construction recipe for an unseen tokenizer family, and a labelled extrapolation probe above 4.2B) or an explicitly narrowed product claim.

WHAT IS NOW SETTLED (iteration 5, reported as measurement, not salvage):

(S1 - THE LOGIT-GAP HARMFUL MARGIN HOLDS, AND IT IS THE FIRST POSITIVE IN FIVE ITERATIONS) [art_Vag73M9ysCVF] The first-decoding-step margin between a per-tokenizer-family empirical refusal-onset lexicon and a continuation lexicon, averaged over the frozen 80-item harmful core - 80 forward passes, ZERO generations, zero judge calls, zero reference models - predicts the judged plain-harmful refusal rate at rho 0.694 [0.495, 0.822] at the member unit (lineage-clustered bootstrap, 10,000 reps) and 0.564 [0.140, 0.826] at the lineage-aggregated unit. Both halves of the pre-registered HOLDS rule (rho >= 0.50 AND CI excluding 0 at BOTH units) are met - the only score in this project ever to do so. The PRE-REGISTERED DECISIVE DIAGNOSTIC, the same one that localised the refit's failure, PASSES: archived block rho 0.6673 vs new block 0.6677, delta -0.0004 [-0.308, 0.380], so the score transfers intact to lineages it was never developed on. Partial Spearman on log10(param_count) is 0.676 [0.475, 0.814] with rho(score, size) only 0.092, so it is not a capability proxy (our-AMS, by contrast, correlates 0.458 with size and its partial drops to 0.243 with a CI covering zero). Leave-one-lineage-out and leave-one-family-out are sign-stable in every fold. Paired against our-AMS sigma on the same resampled lineages: +0.421 [0.169, 0.684], SCORE_BETTER. Monte-Carlo lineage-permutation p sits at the 5e-6 design floor. The benign-regime variant COLLAPSES to 0.129 [-0.168, 0.436] (paired harmful-minus-benign +0.565 [0.205, 0.873]), so the harmful regime is load-bearing and the product claim is 'no generation, no judge, no benchmark, no reference model' - NOT harmful-prompt-free.

(S2 - THE PANEL FIGURE FOR THE PRIMARY SCORE IS 47/24/9, NOT 52/28/11; REVIEWER MAJOR/evidence, CONCEDED) Five members have no empirical refusal-onset lexicon for their tokenizer family and receive NULL primary columns (MISSING_FAMILY_LEXICON, never back-filled). The shipped statistics therefore read n = 47, n_lineages = 24, LOLO 24 folds, LOFO 9 folds, new block 28 members over 18 lineages. Only our-AMS sigma and the union-of-all-families SECONDARY column (rho 0.579 member) are on the full 52/28/11. The contribution survives correction - nine families still exceeds the published maximum of four, and 24 lineage-clustered units remains unmatched in this class - but in a paper whose thesis is that panel size decides conclusions, the drift is the first thing an artifact-opening reader finds. The claim ledger [art_Xx1VPyGi4nAT] audited 911 claims and returned zero flags on the regenerated prose precisely because its pointer schema does not cover per-score n, fold counts, or derived ratio claims; the same blind spot let 'roughly three orders of magnitude cheaper' survive when the compute arithmetic is ~19,000 forward-pass-equivalents against 80, i.e. about two orders, plus the separate and genuinely large removal of judge calls.

(S3 - THE READ-ACT COUPLING IS AN AXIS-TYPE CONTRAST AND IS UNDERPOWERED; H-C ANSWERED) [art_3Nid1IyvhfIG] 169/169 reproduction legs pass at 1e-6, with the pooled rho 0.629 and its CI exact to 0.0. Within axis A across the 14 detection-powered members rho = 0.547, lineage-clustered CI [-0.031, 0.930] over 7 units, exhaustive 5040-permutation p = 0.149; lineage unit 0.821 [0.348, 1.000], same sign. Rank-residualising the axis effect leaves 0.234 [-0.059, 0.397]; removing both main effects leaves 0.126; a mixed-effects slope on ranks gives 0.192. (S4 - THE VERDICT RULE'S ASYMMETRY IS QUANTIFIED, AND THE ABLITERATED ARM NO LONGER NEEDS ANY AUROC; H-K ANSWERED) [art_3Nid1IyvhfIG] Simulating the study's OWN prompt-clustered bootstrap over 141 cells (2,000 replicates x 2,000 inner resamples): at true AUROC 0.500 AT_CHANCE is unreachable below n = 80 per class, P = 0.000 at the pre-registered n = 40 gate (Hanley-McNeil closed form n = 65), while P(READS) = 1.000 under perfect separation at every one of n = 7, 12, 28, 32, 33. The asymmetry is ONE-SIDED: false-READS at true chance is 0.005 at n=10 and 0.001 at n=40, so READS is not noise-driven - it is the NULL verdict that cannot be returned. Tally reported twice: 20 READS / 1 AMBIGUOUS / 0 AT_CHANCE / 9 UNDEFINED over 30; 13/1/0/0 over the 14 powered. DEV-ITER5-01 quotes the code: UNDEFINED fires only on non-finite bootstrap CI bounds (explib.py:486-494 via the >=5-per-class guard), and MIN_PER_CLASS=40 drives a SEPARATE powered flag the verdict never consults - so the Method's '<40 refusals' description was wrong. The abliterated arm is re-carried on refusal RATES with no AUROC: median 0.0076 (weight-edited) and 0.0000 (behavioural-uncensored) vs 0.1131 (aligned); exhaustive permutation over 293,930 assignments p = 0.0026 (scipy's exact method is INVALID under cross-arm ties and its 0.0033 is recorded, never quoted); lineage-clustered median difference -0.1055 [-0.2416, -0.0245]; paired sign test 10/10, p = 0.0020.

(S6 - POSITIONING AND THE THREE PATHOLOGIES ARE PINNED, AND ONE NOVELTY CLAIM IS FORBIDDEN) [art_9sXeYgowURMn] The 2506.24056 coverage conflict is not version drift: 97.5-99.8% (aligned gap exceeds base gap) and 92-99% (position-1 decision census) are both in v2 and measure different things. HURTADO: H-G may NOT claim 'first model-level cheap safety score validated against behaviour' - his labels come from a behavioural oracle (Qwen3Guard), not a provenance tag; the four surviving residuals are attested-reference-required, BINARY vs graded, 4 families vs 9, and full weight download. Canonical sources pinned and quoted: leakage = Kapoor & Narayanan [L3.3] sampling bias in test distribution; aggregation = Robinson 1950 (+.118 individual, -.526 over 48 states, -.619 over 9 divisions) with Openshaw CATMOG 38 ('for a 6 region aggregation of the 99 Iowa counties the range of possible correlations is between -.99 and +.99'); small-sample = Schoenbrodt & Perugini 2013 Table 1 (POS_crit 252/238/212/181 at rho .1-.4), so n = 24-28 lineages is 6.5-9x below stability and this must be stated deliberately. The reference-class tally (5 of 5 model-level scores validate at <=4 families, 4 of 5 at <=14 checkpoints, 0 of 5 resample on the lineage) rests on an arXiv-scoped search plus five harvested related-work sections, with 13 scholarly-mode zeros explicitly NOT credible - so it is a located sample, not an enumeration.

THE REVISED CLAIMS, in the order they must now be tested:

(H-T - IS THE HEADLINE A NEAR-TAUTOLOGY? THE NEW PRIMARY TEST; reviewer MAJOR/methodology) The score and the outcome are read on the SAME 80 in_core80 harmful prompts, and this project's own dataset artifact established that refusal onset is near a one-token event. The natural sceptical reading of rho = 0.694 is therefore 'the first-token refusal-vs-continuation margin on prompt X predicts whether the greedy generation on prompt X opens with a refusal opener'. Two comparisons settle it and both are nearly free. (a) PROMPT-DISJOINT CRITERION: the frozen manifest ships 594 plain_harmful items and the score is read on 80, so judge a stratified sample of the 514 non-core items on the panel (the judge pipeline and cache already exist) and report score-vs-behaviour rho at both units on items with ZERO overlap with the scoring set. (b) ARGMAX BASELINE: from the very same 80 forward passes, take the greedy first token and score the fraction landing in the refusal-onset lexicon; report it as a row of the headline table with the same paired lineage bootstrap used against our-AMS, and state explicitly whether the continuous margin beats it. PRE-REGISTER: (i) rho on prompt-disjoint items >= 0.50 with a lineage-clustered CI excluding 0 at the member unit; (ii) paired delta (margin - argmax) with its CI, and a pre-committed statement that a delta whose CI covers 0 means the product claim must be restated around the VALIDATION rather than the scalar. Then add the mechanism paragraph the paper never wrote: the score is a first-token statistic and the outcome is largely first-token-determined, so say what the residual 1 - rho^2 consists of (hedge-then-comply, mid-generation refusal, degenerate output) and which class of checkpoint the score is therefore structurally blind to. Answering this pre-emptively is worth more than any further robustness check.

(H-R - ESTABLISH THE CEILING BEFORE INTERPRETING 0.694; reviewer MAJOR/rigor) The outcome carries a REFUSAL one-vs-rest annotator kappa of 0.3907 against a 0.60 threshold, from an LLM panel whose frozen predecessor reproduced its own labels only 75% of the time [art_gYmQllaTCGT5]. A reader currently cannot tell whether 0.694 is a weak predictor of a good label or a good predictor of a noisy one - which is the difference between ship and do-not-ship. Re-judge one block of archived generations under a fresh seed and report the member-level rho between the two judged rate vectors as the ATTAINABLE MAXIMUM for any score on this panel; then either report the headline relative to that ceiling or, if the ceiling is well above 0.694, say so and attribute the gap to the score rather than the label. Simultaneously DROP the disattenuated column or mark it INADMISSIBLE: the shipped disattenuated member-level value is 1.110, outside the parameter space, which is the standard signature of a degenerate correction at this reliability, and presenting it as a companion statistic implies it is informative.

(H-D - MAKE THE PRODUCT CLAIM MEET THE EVIDENCE; reviewer MAJOR/scope) 'A platform could run this tomorrow' is not licensed by measurements confined to 0.13B-4.2B, a score that requires the target's tokenizer family to already have an empirical refusal-onset lexicon (5 of 52 panel members did not, with no procedure for a family that arrives tomorrow), and a rank with no operating threshold. Close the cheapest gap and narrow the rest. CHEAPEST AND HIGHEST VALUE: report PRECISION AND RECALL at one concrete decision rule (e.g. flag the bottom quartile by margin) so a deployer can price the errors, with the false-negative rate stated. SECOND: give the lexicon-construction recipe as a costed procedure - the dataset artifact already derives lexicons behaviourally from greedy rollouts, so state how many rollouts a new family needs and what it costs, which is the difference between a metric and a research result. THIRD, optional: one 7-8B lineage triad as a LABELLED extrapolation probe. If none of these is done, the framing must become 'a score whose criterion validity is established at <=4.2B across nine architecture families, with deployment at frontier scale untested'.

(H-P2 - RESTATE EVERY BREADTH FIGURE AND EXTEND THE AUDITOR; reviewer MAJOR/evidence and MINOR/evidence) Restate the primary score's panel as 47 members / 24 lineages / 9 families, its jackknives as 24 LOLO and 9 LOFO folds, the new block as 28 members over 18 lineages, and 'transfers to 21 lineages' as 18, with one sentence in both Preliminaries and the results section stating that the five MISSING_FAMILY_LEXICON members remove three lineages and two families from the PRIMARY panel while our-AMS and the union-lexicon secondary remain on 52/28/11. Adjust the contribution to 'nine architecture families against the published maximum of four'. Replace 'roughly three orders of magnitude cheaper' with the explicit arithmetic (80 forward passes against roughly 19,000 forward-pass-equivalents of generation, plus several hundred judge calls removed entirely). EXTEND the claim ledger's pointer schema to cover per-score sample sizes, fold counts, and derived ratio claims, re-run it, and only then may 'zero flags' do rhetorical work.

(H-S - PUBLISH THE REFERENCE-CLASS SEARCH PROTOCOL; reviewer MINOR/novelty) The conjunction claim is only as strong as the completeness of the reference class, and 'five located scores' is currently presented as an enumeration when the dossier's own saturation section concedes 13 uninformative scholarly-mode searches. Report the protocol - query strings, venues and date range, the inclusion criterion for 'model-level internal safety score', candidates screened vs included - and restate as 'of the N model-level internal safety scores locatable under the protocol in Appendix B, none resamples on the weight lineage'.

(H-N2 - PROMOTE THE CONTROL FLOORS, DEMOTE THE UNDERPOWERED NULL; reviewer MINOR/rigor) Two measurements generalise beyond this paper and are currently under-emphasised: a matched random direction induces refusal >= 0.10 on 7 of 30 members (worst 0.389, median 0.028), and the 20-draw random READING band spans +/-0.075 to +/-0.500 across members because residual streams are anisotropic - so 'chance is 0.500' and 'one random draw is a null' are both unsafe. Promote these to a named contribution ('two empirical control floors any steering study should adopt'); compress the read-act coupling material to one paragraph plus the decomposition table.

(H-A2 - WRITE THE ABSTRACT AND STOP WRITING A CHANGELOG; reviewer MINOR/clarity) The draft has no abstract while referring to one twice. Write it (corrected panel figures, headline rho, cost line, the not-harmful-prompt-free honesty statement, the two retractions). Then convert every reviewer-addressed and iteration-addressed sentence ('our previous draft', 'the reviewer's objection was', 'iteration 4') into a direct statement of result; a reader of the submitted paper has seen none of that history, and the current phrasing converts strong results into apologetics for claims that were never made to them.

(H-N - NAME THE PHENOMENA, THEN CLAIM THE INSTANCE; unchanged, sources now pinned) Item-pool provenance is leakage [L3.3]; the aggregation unit moving our-AMS rho from 0.358 (19 members) to 0.821 (7 lineages), a gap of 0.4636, is ecological correlation in Robinson's sense; the 7->28 lineage collapse is small-sample instability. Claim the instances, never the phenomena, and name which pair of cells each number comes from - the 0.464 gap, the 0.553 oriented-Delta difference, and the median 0.238 / max 0.557 / 5-of-16 sign flips are THREE DIFFERENT quantities that the previous draft blurred.

(H-U, H-P - DONE, NO FURTHER WORK) Dual aggregation is complete [art__tq3ZgPRYB0B] and must be extended verbatim to whatever H-T produces. The five-check protocol remains a limitations instrument, never a certification protocol, its negative robust on 1.0000 of a 164,736-point grid and located in the verdict-class and interiority clauses rather than the cutoffs.

WHAT THIS PROJECT NOW CLAIMS, plainly: (1) a benchmark-free, harmful-prompt-free ACT-SIDE safety score does not follow from steering strength along a refusal axis, and alpha_50 loses to a cheaper forward-pass scanner under every unit and carrier computable; (2) ONE cheap forward-pass score - the first-decoding-step logit-gap harmful margin, 80 forward passes and zero generations - predicts judged plain-harmful refusal at rho 0.694 [0.495, 0.822] over 47 members / 24 lineages / 9 families, transfers across provenance blocks at 0.667 vs 0.668, is not a size proxy, and beats our-AMS by +0.421 - but whether it is more than a restatement of the first generated token remains the open question that H-T settles, and its reliability ceiling is unmeasured; (3) the same panel retires the AMS paraphrase refit, which makes the positive interpretable rather than merely large: at seven lineages the two scores are indistinguishable and at twenty-four to twenty-eight they are not; (4) reading and steering along one refusal axis are NOT dissociated, but the strength of the coupling is unresolved and underpowered, and the axis reads canonically-worded refusals only; (5) two empirical control floors and three named measurement pathologies, each measured on this study's own published numbers. The three controlled negatives (bistable/EWS; alpha_50; the protocol) stand as contributions in their own right.
