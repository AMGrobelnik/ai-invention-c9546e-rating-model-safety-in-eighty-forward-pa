**5.1 What the refusal axis reads, and what the normalisation buys it.** The detection result of iteration 4 is a re-analysis of 30 scored checkpoints, of which 11 are both detection-powered (>= 40 spontaneous items per class) and carry the stored projections this analysis needs; every archived per-member AUROC, its prompt-clustered CI and the paired A-B difference regenerate from those stores to 0.0e+00 across 667 cells before any number below is computed. The detection label and the axis share a lexical basis: axis A is the diff-in-means of hand-written canned refusals against canned compliances, and the label of record is an anchored regex over canned-refusal openers, so part of any AUROC they share is definitional.  Measured on 613 re-labelled items over 11 powered members, the two criteria agree at Cohen's kappa +0.789 [+0.699, +0.879], and the semantic rubric finds 83 REFUSAL_NONCANONICAL items, of which 29 are ones the regex calls non-refusals (38 counting both refusal classes; weighted corpus prevalence 0.0546). Re-labelling 660 stratified items with the five-class semantic rubric ($0.0277 of a $2.00 cap, cumulative over every run of this artifact; the cache makes a rerun free) moves the axis-A AUROC from 0.834 [0.736, 0.923] under the regex label to 0.821 [0.752, 0.866] under the semantic one, a paired difference of -0.013 [-0.067, +0.030] at the member level and -0.024 [-0.066, +0.018] at the lineage level. The deciding split is the 83 genuine refusals the regex misses (REFUSAL_NONCANONICAL; weighted corpus prevalence 0.124 [0.084, 0.181]): the axis separates them from compliances at AUROC 0.611 [0.542, 0.686], against 0.897 [0.864, 0.922] on canonically-worded ones and a measured 20-draw random reading band whose upper edge averages 0.750 (chance is NOT 0.500 here). The rubric's own CANONICAL/NON-CANONICAL split is not the regex's split, so the sharper subset is the 38 items the regex actually calls non-refusals while the rubric calls them refusals (29 of them REFUSAL_NONCANONICAL; weighted corpus prevalence 0.0546 [0.0412, 0.0686]): on those the axis reaches AUROC 0.576 [0.371, 0.728] (UNDERPOWERED). The pre-registered verdict is READS_CANONICAL_WORDING_ONLY at the member level and READS_CANONICAL_WORDING_ONLY at the lineage level. Applying the one published leakage control (arXiv:2607.13346) to our own headline -- re-estimating every centring and scaling statistic inside the training fold under leave-one-prompt-out, rather than on the whole scored pool as the archived readout does -- moves axis-A AUROC by -0.0205 [-0.0352, -0.0071] (centring alone: +0.0009; the deliberately leaky whole-pool z-score: -0.0205), against the -0.336 that control produced on its author's own data. The same protocol moves the norm-matched random axis D by -0.0020 [-0.0084, +0.0032] and axis B by -0.0023, which is the control on the control. Verdict LEAKAGE_CONTROL_SMALL_DELTA (member level) / LEAKAGE_CONTROL_SMALL_DELTA (lineage level).
