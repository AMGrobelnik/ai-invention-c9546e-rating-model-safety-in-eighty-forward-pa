E. σ vs. Behavioral Compliance
For each of the 14 models we measure behavioral com-
pliance on 20 stratified JailbreakBench prompts. Compliance
rate ranges from 0.05 (gemma-2-9b-it) to 1.00 (gemma-2-9b-
it-abliterated). Figure 1 shows the relationship between σharmful
and compliance rate.
We measure two correlations: Pearson (linear) and Spear-
man (rank). Using σharmful:
• Pearson r = −0.546, p = 0.043 (n = 14). Significant at
α = 0.05.
• Spearman ρ = −0.423, p = 0.13. Not significant.
Using the worst-concept σmin across the three concepts
produces slightly weaker correlations (Pearson r = −0.505,
p = 0.065; Spearman ρ = −0.273, p = 0.34). The harmful-
content concept is the better behavioral predictor.
Three observations follow. First, the per-category cluster-
ing on the scatter is clean: instruction-tuned models cluster
lower-right (high σ, low compliance) and uncensored/base
models cluster upper-left (low σ, high compliance). The
basic structural-behavioral relationship is real. Second, the
three off-trend high-compliance points at high σ (DarkIdol
at (5.45, 0.97), gemma-2-9b-it-abliterated at (4.54, 1.00), and
Llama-3.1-Inst.-abliterated at (3.33, 0.93)) flatten the linear
relationship. Third, the structural signal alone is therefore
not a sufficient behavioral predictor for safety certification.
AMS scores must be combined with behavioral evaluation;
we discuss this in Section IX.
F. Layer Sweep on False-Negative Models
AMS selects a single optimal layer per concept in the 40–
80% depth band. To test whether the two Tier-1 false negatives
(DarkIdol and gemma-2-9b-it-abliterated) might be caught by
scanning a different layer, we compute σharmful at every layer
in the wider 20–90% depth band for the two false-negative
models and two controls (gemma-2-9b-it as a PASS control
and dolphin-2.9-llama3-8b as a CRITICAL control). Figure 2
shows the resulting layer profiles.
DarkIdol’s σ remains in [4.01, 5.45] across the entire 20–
90% depth band—no layer choice exposes it. gemma-2-9b-it-
abliterated dips to 3.12σ at the shallowest swept layer (L8,
depth 0.20), but the clean PASS-band control gemma-2-9b-it
reaches its own minimum (3.21σ) at the same layer. Across
the full sweep the two curves are nearly indistinguishable.
Both spend the same 2/30 layers below 3.5σ and stay above
3.0σ everywhere else. A min-σ-across-layers rule that flagged
gemma-abliterated would also flag gemma-clean. Multi-layer
scanning is therefore not a viable fix for either false negative.
The failure mode is structural, not a layer-selection artifact.
The CRITICAL control (dolphin-2.9) stays at σ ≤1.4
at every swept layer, confirming the layer-sweep estimator
produces sensibly low values when the underlying geometry
really is broken.

Class (i) — Training removal. The modification removes
safety training entirely, either via fine-tuning from base on
unfiltered data (Dolphin) or by serving the base model without
instruction tuning at all. Without safety training, the model
never learned to represent harmful and benign content in
distinct activation regions. The contrastive separation collapses
below 1.5σ. Tier 1 catches this case cleanly. This is the
canonical case AMS was originally designed for.
Class (ii) — Weight-orthogonalization abliteration. The
textbook abliteration technique [1] identifies the refusal di-
rection and orthogonalizes the residual-stream-writing weight
matrices against it. The model is rewritten so the original
refusal axis is no longer expressible in the residual stream.
This both collapses the cluster separation and rotates whatever
residual refusal direction can still be extracted. mlabonn
e/Meta-Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct-abliterated
exemplifies this class: σharmful drops from 5.67 (clean baseline)
to 3.33, and the per-layer refusal direction cosine similarity
drops to a minimum of 0.30 (Section VIII-B). Tier 1 flags
this as WARNING; Tier 2 fails verification. Defense in depth.
Class (iii) — Rotation-without-collapse abliteration. A
different abliteration procedure, used in cases where standard
orthogonalization fails on the target architecture, rotates the
refusal direction without collapsing the cluster geometry. Il
yaGusev/gemma-2-9b-it-abliterated exemplifies
this class. The uploader explicitly reports that standard or-
thogonalization “did not produce the same results” on Gemma
2 due to its RMSNorm-before-residual-merge architecture,
and substituted TransformerLens-style interventions with an
embedding-layer rescaling step. The resulting signature is
mild: mean per-layer cosine similarity 0.84, minimum 0.70,
∆-norm averaging 0.52—far from the orthogonal limit. Cluster
separation is preserved at σ = 4.54. Tier 1 misses this case;

leaves both magnitude and direction of the refusal vector
substantially intact while modifying behavioral output via fine-
tuning that re-weights the refusal-conditional generation dis-

sored exemplifies this class. σharmful = 5.45 across all swept
layers (Section VII-F). The refusal direction is preserved;
the contrastive cluster is preserved; yet the model complies
with 19 of 20 stratified harmful prompts (97% compliance).
This case is undetectable by activation-only probing of mid-
residual-stream representations. The exact training procedure
is not publicly documented but is consistent with one of:
DPO on harmful-comply pairs that adjusts output-layer logits
without rewriting middle-layer features; targeted fine-tuning
of the final transformer block or lm_head; or instruction-
following over-fitting that conditions the model to suppress
refusal tokens at decode time. We do not claim to have verified
the specific mechanism; we report what is consistent with the
observed signature and leave verification for future work.
