D. Layer Selection
Safety-relevant information is not uniformly distributed
across layers. Following representation engineering literature
[3], [16], we perform a calibration sweep across layers in
the 40-80% depth range, selecting the layer with maximum
separation. This typically requires ∼15 forward passes and
completes in under 30 seconds.
E. Two-Tier Detection
a) Tier 1: Generic Safety Check: Measures whether any
safety directions exist, without requiring a baseline. We define
three safety levels based on separation:
• PASS (>3.5σ): Safety directions intact
• WARNING (2.0-3.5σ): Weak/degraded safety directions
• CRITICAL (<2.0σ): No safety training detected
These thresholds were calibrated on our validation data:
instruction-tuned models show 3.8-8.4σ, abliterated models
show 3.3σ, and the tested uncensored models show 1.1-
1.3σ. The 3.5σ PASS threshold ensures abliterated models are
flagged as WARNING rather than passing.
Tier 1 catches completely degraded models (uncensored
fine-tunes, base models without safety training) and partially
degraded models (abliteration).
